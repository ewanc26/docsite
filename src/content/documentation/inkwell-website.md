[inkwell-website](https://github.com/ewanc26/inkwell-website) is the landing page and OAuth metadata host for the [Inkwell iOS app](https://github.com/ewanc26/inkwell) — a native reader and writer for the Standard.site publishing ecosystem on AT Protocol.

## Purpose

The website serves two primary functions:

1. **Landing page** — Public-facing site providing information about Inkwell, download links, and documentation.
2. **OAuth metadata host** — Hosts the OAuth client metadata and redirect URIs required for AT Protocol OAuth sign-in, enabling the Inkwell app to authenticate users via their AT Protocol handle.
